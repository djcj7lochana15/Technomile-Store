# Techno Mile Store - Official eBay Showcase Website

Modern, responsive, high-converting multi-page e-commerce storefront for **Techno Mile Store** (Seller: **Lochana**).

---

## 🌟 Pages Overview
- **`index.html`**: Home page with hero section, trust metrics, featured categories, top selling eBay products, owner spotlight, and eBay Buyer Protection banner.
- **`products.html`**: Full catalog with instant live search, category pills (Fashion & Bags, Beauty & Care, Car & Travel, Home & Lifestyle, Tech), price/rating sorting, and product cards.
- **`product-detail.html`**: Dedicated product view with thumbnail image gallery, detailed specifications, direct "Buy on eBay" button, and WhatsApp inquiry.
- **`about.html`**: The story of Techno Mile Store (active on eBay since 2012), 100% positive seller feedback, and founder profile featuring Lochana.
- **`contact.html`**: Dedicated Contact Us page featuring Lochana's photo, direct phone, email, Katugastota registered address, interactive inquiry form, WhatsApp shortcut, and Google Maps embed.

---

## 🚀 Key Features
- **No Node.js / NPM Required**: Pure HTML5, Tailwind CSS (via CDN), custom CSS, and vanilla JavaScript. Simply double-click `index.html` in your file explorer to open it in any browser!
- **Official eBay Integration**: Direct links to `https://www.ebay.com.au/usr/technomilestore` on every product card, modal, and banner.
- **Interactive Inquiry Basket & Wishlist**: Add items to cart/wishlist saved in `localStorage`.
- **One-Click WhatsApp Ordering**: Generates pre-formatted WhatsApp messages to `+94719215916` with the selected items and total estimated value.
- **Multi-Currency Switcher**: Real-time price switching between **AUD (AU$)**, **USD ($)**, and **LKR (Rs.)**.
- **Quick View Modals**: Preview items without leaving the catalog.
